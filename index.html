How it works

From YouTube link to TikTok draft in 4 steps

No editing software, no manual export, no re-upload. The full workflow takes about 30 seconds.

Login with TikTok

Authenticate once with your TikTok account using the official OAuth flow. Grant the scopes shown.

Paste a YouTube URL

Pick any public YouTube video and choose the start and end of the segment you want to clip.

nina1pp processes it

The video is downloaded, cut to the chosen segment, and reformatted to vertical 1080×1920.

Publish from your phone

The clip lands in your TikTok inbox as a draft. Open the app, add caption and hashtags, and post.

The actual processing pipeline

This is what happens under the hood when you click "Generate clip":

$ nina1pp clip --url "youtube.com/watch?v=..." --start 30 --end 60 [1/4] downloading source video with yt-dlp ... [2/4] cutting segment 30s → 60s with ffmpeg ... [3/4] reformatting to 9:16 with blurred background ... [4/4] uploading to TikTok inbox via Content Posting API ... ✓ sent to your TikTok inbox. publish_id: v_inbox_file~v2.7634049... ✓ open TikTok on your phone to review and publish.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does nina1pp publish to my TikTok automatically?
No. Clips are uploaded to your TikTok inbox as drafts. You always open the TikTok mobile app and press publish yourself. nina1pp never posts on your behalf without explicit user action.
What permissions does nina1pp request?
Two TikTok scopes: user.info.basic (to identify you after login) and video.upload (to send the clip to your inbox as a draft). We never request access to your followers, your videos list, or your DMs.
Where are my YouTube videos and clips stored?
Source videos are deleted right after the clip is generated. Generated clips are deleted within 24 hours. We do not back up, share, or analyze your content. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Do I need to install anything?
No. nina1pp runs entirely in the browser on the web side, and on the TikTok app on your phone for the publish step. No browser extensions, no desktop software.
Can I revoke nina1pp's access?
Yes, anytime. Open the TikTok app → Settings → Security & Permissions → Manage app permissions → Revoke nina1pp. Your access tokens become invalid immediately.
Is this affiliated with TikTok?
No. nina1pp is an independent tool that uses TikTok's official public Developer APIs (Login Kit and Content Posting API). It is not endorsed by or affiliated with TikTok.
Can I clip private or unlisted YouTube videos?
Only videos that are publicly accessible via YouTube can be processed. nina1pp does not bypass YouTube's privacy or copyright protections. You are responsible for ensuring you have the rights to the content you clip.
