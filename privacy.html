Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how nina1pp ("we", "our", "the Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Service. By using nina1pp, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok account information. When you authenticate with TikTok via OAuth, we receive your TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar URL through the user.info.basic scope.
  • OAuth tokens. TikTok issues access and refresh tokens that allow us to upload video drafts to your TikTok account on your behalf. These tokens are stored encrypted on our servers and used only for the operations you initiate.
  • Source video metadata. The YouTube URL you submit, along with the timestamps you choose for clipping. We do not retain the downloaded source video after the clip is generated.
  • Generated clips. Clips you produce are stored temporarily on our servers only as long as needed to upload them to your TikTok account, and are then deleted.
  • Operational logs. Standard server logs (IP address, timestamps, request paths) used for diagnosing errors and protecting against abuse. These are kept for no more than 30 days.

2. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you with TikTok via OAuth.
  • To upload clips you generate to your own TikTok account, as drafts, using the video.upload scope.
  • To maintain the operation, security, and integrity of the Service.
  • To respond to your direct inquiries and support requests.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your personal information with third parties.
  • We do not publish content to your TikTok account without your explicit confirmation in the TikTok mobile app.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or training of machine learning models.
  • We do not access your TikTok DMs, followers list, payment information, or any other data outside the user.info.basic and video.upload scopes.

4. Data retention

Source videos and generated clips are deleted from our servers within 24 hours of clip generation. OAuth access tokens are retained until you disconnect your TikTok account or delete your nina1pp account, after which they are revoked and erased. Operational logs are retained for at most 30 days.

5. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Disconnect your TikTok account from nina1pp by revoking access in your TikTok account settings → Security & Permissions → Manage app permissions.
  • Request the deletion of all data associated with your account by emailing us at test8902026@gmail.com. We will action the request within 14 days.
  • Request a copy of any personal information we hold about you.

6. Third parties

The Service interacts with TikTok and YouTube. Their handling of your data is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy

nina1pp does not control or take responsibility for the privacy practices of these third parties.

7. Security

OAuth tokens and credentials are stored encrypted at rest. All communication between the user, nina1pp, and TikTok is protected by HTTPS/TLS. We follow industry-standard security practices, but no system is 100% secure; you use the Service at your own risk.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, contact us immediately so we can delete it.

9. International transfers

Our servers may be located outside your country of residence. By using the Service, you consent to your data being transferred to and processed in those jurisdictions, subject to appropriate safeguards.
