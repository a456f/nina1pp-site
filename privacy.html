Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how nina1pp ("we", "our", "the Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Service.

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok account information — when you authenticate with TikTok via OAuth, we receive your TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar URL through the user.info.basic scope.
  • Access and refresh tokens — TikTok issues OAuth access and refresh tokens that allow us to upload video drafts to your TikTok account on your behalf. These tokens are stored securely on our servers.
  • Source video metadata — the YouTube URL you submit, along with the timestamps you choose for clipping. We do not retain the downloaded source video after the clip is generated.
  • Generated clips — clips you produce are stored temporarily on our servers only as long as needed to upload them to your TikTok account.

2. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you with TikTok.
  • To upload the clip you generate to your own TikTok account, as a draft, using the video.upload scope.
  • To maintain the operation, security, and integrity of the Service.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.
  • We do not publish content to your TikTok account without your explicit confirmation in the TikTok mobile app.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the Service.

4. Data retention

Source videos and generated clips are deleted from our servers within 24 hours of clip generation. OAuth tokens are retained until you disconnect your TikTok account or delete your nina1pp account, after which they are revoked and erased.

5. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • Disconnect your TikTok account from nina1pp by revoking access in your TikTok account settings.
  • Request the deletion of all data associated with your account by emailing test8902026@gmail.com.

6. Third parties

The Service interacts with TikTok and YouTube. Their handling of your data is governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy

7. Security

OAuth tokens and credentials are stored encrypted at rest. All communication between the user, nina1pp, and TikTok is protected by HTTPS/TLS.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
